# Billing, finance and insurance claims

## Cashier

- **Shift Start / Close**: open your shift with the float in the drawer; close it by counting cash.
  Differences are recorded.
- **Invoices**: bills are created automatically from care (consultation, tests, scans, drugs,
  procedures, ward days). Take payment by cash, mobile money, card or transfer; a receipt is issued.
- Refunds and write-offs need a reason and are recorded.
- A bill that is being claimed from insurance cannot take cash payment until the claim is settled
  or withdrawn.

## Finance

**Float Tracker**, **Expenses**, **Account Ledger** and **Billing Analytics** give the money
picture; **Reports** has daily and monthly summaries.

## Insurance and NHIS claims

**Claims → Insurance Claims**:

1. **Schemes**: NHIS and private insurers.
2. **Memberships**: each patient's scheme, member number and expiry. An expired membership cannot
   be claimed.
3. **Claims** are prepared from completed visits, with each item's tariff code.
4. **Batches**: group a month's claims and submit.
5. Record the scheme's decision per line (paid, reduced, rejected, query), then the payment
   received.
