# Maternity, child health and clinics

## Maternity

- **Maternity Register**: each pregnancy (one ongoing per woman), with gestation worked out from
  the last menstrual period or a dating scan.
- **Antenatal Clinic** / **Postnatal Clinic**: visits are recorded on a clinic visit. Danger signs
  (for example very high blood pressure) raise an alert.
- **Delivery**: record the birth; every live-born baby is registered as a patient, linked to the
  mother.

## Child health

- **Child Welfare Clinic**: growth (weight, length, MUAC) with alerts when growth falters.
- **Immunisations** follow the Ghana EPI schedule. LHIMS checks the order and the minimum gap
  between doses, and refuses expired vaccine batches. A dose given in error is voided, not deleted.
- **Immunisation Due List**: children due or overdue — use it to follow up defaulters.

## Specialty clinics

Dental, Eye, Physiotherapy and Dietetics have their own clinic queue. Each visit has a structured
form (saved as a record that cannot be edited later; corrections are added):

- **Dental**: tooth-by-tooth chart (FDI numbering) and treatment.
- **Eye**: visual acuity, eye pressure, examination and refraction.
- **Physiotherapy**: assessment, goals and sessions.
- **Dietetics**: BMI and MUAC are calculated for you; diet plan and follow-up.
