# Reception

## Registering a new patient

**Reception → Walk-Ins → Register**: full name, date of birth, gender, phone, and if known the
national ID, insurance and allergies. **Register Walk-in & Request Tests** saves the patient; you can
then add lab tests or scans straight away, or stop there.

Search first (**Patient Records**) to avoid registering someone twice. Possible duplicates are
listed under **Walk-Ins → Duplicates** for review.

## Sending a patient to be seen

- **OPD Visits**: find the patient and start a visit (choose the consultation fee if your facility
  charges one). The patient goes to triage, then the doctor.
- Specialty clinics (antenatal, dental, eye, …) have their own queue under their department.
- **Emergency Board**: for patients who cannot be identified, register them quickly with what you
  know; correct the record later.

## Doctor orders and check-in

- **Incoming Orders**: tests ordered by doctors (including outside doctors) wait here. Confirm them
  and check the patient in when they arrive.
- **Patient Check-In**: mark arrivals for booked orders.

## Appointments and the day's log

- **Appointments**: book, move or cancel.
- **Daily Visit Log**: everyone seen today.
- **Results Inbox**: released results waiting to be handed over or sent.

## Rules the system enforces

- A patient recorded as deceased cannot be given a new visit.
- A cancelled visit cannot be used for notes or charges.
