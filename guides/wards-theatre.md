# Wards and theatre

## Wards

**Wards → Ward Board** shows every ward and bed: free, occupied, or being cleaned.

- **Admit**: from a visit (the visit closes as "admitted" and its diagnoses carry over), or a
  planned admission. One patient per bed, and one active admission per patient.
- **Transfer** to another bed or ward; the bed history is kept.
- **Medication chart**: for each dose, mark given, held, refused or missed (with a reason).
- **Nursing and progress notes** during the stay.
- **Discharge** with a summary. Ward charges are added by midnight count. The bed then needs
  cleaning before the next patient.
- An admission made in error can be cancelled until care has been recorded on it.

## Theatre

**Theatre → Theatre List**: operations booked per theatre and time.

- **Book** an operation on an open visit or ward stay. A theatre cannot be double-booked.
- The **WHO Surgical Safety Checklist** is done in three steps: *sign in* (before anaesthesia),
  *time out* (before incision), *sign out* (before the patient leaves). Each step must be completed
  in order.
- The **operation note** is written into the patient's record; the procedure is billed at sign out.
- If a booked patient is admitted, their operation moves onto the ward stay automatically.
