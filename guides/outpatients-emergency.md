# Outpatients (OPD) and emergency

## The visit, step by step

1. **Triage (nurse)** — **OPD Visits**, open the waiting patient, record vital signs and the triage
   level.
2. **Consultation (doctor)** — start the consultation, then record:
   - notes (history, examination, assessment, plan);
   - **diagnoses** (ICD-10 search; mark the main one);
   - **allergies** — recorded once, shown on every later visit;
   - **prescriptions** — each drug with dose, how often and for how long;
   - lab tests and scans.
3. **Close the visit** with the outcome: sent home, admitted, referred, or died.

## Prescribing safely

If a drug matches a recorded allergy (the drug itself or its class), LHIMS stops you. You may
continue only by giving a reason, which is recorded.

## Corrections

Clinical notes are never overwritten. To correct one, add a correction; the original stays visible
with who changed it and when.

## Emergency Board

- The board lists everyone in the emergency department by triage level, most urgent first.
- **Unidentified patients** can be registered with a description (for example "unknown man, about
  40"); complete their details when known.
- Admit, refer or discharge from the patient's visit as in OPD.
