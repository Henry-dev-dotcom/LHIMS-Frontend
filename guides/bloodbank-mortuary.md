# Blood bank and mortuary

## Blood bank

- **Donors**: eligibility is checked (for example the gap since the last donation); ineligible donors
  can be deferred with a reason.
- **Donations** are split into components, each with its own shelf life. Units stay in
  **quarantine** until all screening tests are negative.
- **Requests** from the wards → **crossmatch** (ABO and Rh compatibility is checked) → the unit is
  reserved → **issue**.
- **Emergency release**: O-negative can be issued before crossmatch in an emergency; the reason is
  recorded.
- **Transfusions** are recorded with any reaction. Expired units are discarded.

## Mortuary

- **Deceased register**: each body gets a tag and a storage slot (one body per slot).
- A doctor **certifies** the death; police cases need **police clearance** before release.
- **Release** only to an identified collector; storage charges are billed.
- Once a patient is recorded as deceased, LHIMS refuses new visits and admissions for them.
